How to play

Nonogram rules

  • Each clue gives the lengths of filled groups in that row or column.
  • Separate consecutive groups with at least one empty cell.
  • Use × to mark cells you know are empty.
  • Complete the puzzle by finding every filled cell.

About the project

These puzzles were used to compare SAT-solver statistics with human difficulty ratings and solving behaviour. Personal bests stay on this device; no gameplay data is collected. Read the research paper or view the research poster.
